The Parva Assessment

The questions come after the story. Once you have walked all eighteen Parvas, answer eighteen questions, one from each Parva, in the order of the story. Answer correctly on the first try for full marks. Get one wrong and Dronacharya sends you back to the exact moment in the timeline to learn it again. Then you return to the same question.

  • 10 points: correct on the first try
  • 5 points: correct after one review
  • 2 points: correct after two or more reviews
  • +3 streak bonus for every 3 first-try answers in a row
